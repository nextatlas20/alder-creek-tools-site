# Alder Creek Tool Lending Library

Our mission: lend tools, teach repair skills, and keep usable equipment out of landfill.

- Address: 418 Millrace Road, Alder Creek
- Opening hours: Tue-Thu 4-8 pm, Sat 9 am-1 pm
- Loan period: 7 days
- Late fee: $2 per day after the due date
- Public contact email: hello@aldercreektools.example
