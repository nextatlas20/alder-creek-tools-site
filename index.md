# Alder Creek Tool Library

We lend tools to our neighbors and help keep repairable equipment out of the landfill.

- Address: 418 Millrace Road, Alder Creek
- Hours: Tue/Thu 5-8 pm
- Loan period: 14 days
- Late fee: $1 per day

Tools available for loan: 4

Questions? Write to info@aldercreektools.example.
